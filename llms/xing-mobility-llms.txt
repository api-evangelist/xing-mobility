# XING Mobility

> XING Mobility (行競科技) is a Taiwan-based battery technology company that builds
> immersion-cooled lithium battery systems under the IMMERSIO brand — cell-to-pack
> (CTP) packs, battery backup units (BBU) for AI data centers, and the XBE series
> for stationary and mobile energy storage — serving electric mobility, ESS,
> marine, and data-center markets. XING Mobility publishes no public developer
> API, developer portal, or API documentation as of 2026-07-21.
